Privacy notice and terms

Last updated 20 October 2026

What this website collects

Nothing is stored by us when you browse this website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising, and loads no code, fonts or images from any other company. Every file you receive comes from falaj.mraisi.com.

The demonstration form prepares a message on your own device. Nothing reaches us until you choose to send that message yourself through WhatsApp or email. At that point we receive the details you entered: your name, organisation, phone number, email address and any note you added.

How we use your details

We use them only to reply to you and arrange the demonstration you asked for, on the basis of the consent you give when you send the request. We do not sell your details, share them with other organisations for their own purposes, or add you to marketing lists.

How long we keep them

We keep an enquiry while we are discussing the demonstration with you and, if we go on to work together, for as long as that relationship requires. Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within twelve months. You can ask us to delete yours sooner at any time.

Services involved

If you send your request by WhatsApp or email, that message is carried by the service you chose (for example WhatsApp, operated by Meta, or your email provider) under its own privacy terms. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may record technical data such as IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes, as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Information kept on your device

The only thing this website saves in your browser is your choice of language, so that the page opens in Arabic or English next time. It stays on your device and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Your rights

Under Oman's Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree 6/2022) you may withdraw your consent, ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct, transfer or delete it. Write to mahmood@mraisi.com and we will respond within the period the law requires. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology.

Security

The website is served only over an encrypted connection and allows the browser to run only its own verified scripts. To report a security concern, please contact mahmood@mraisi.com.
